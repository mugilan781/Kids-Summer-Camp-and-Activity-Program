Your Family's Privacy Is Sacred Ground

The same care we give your kids on the climbing wall, we give your data on our servers. Here's exactly what that means.

Effective: July 1, 2026 Version 4.2 COPPA compliant GDPR-ready

01Who We Are

CampQuest Adventures ("CampQuest", "we", "us") operates a summer camp and activity program at 4821 Lakeshore Trail, Boulder Creek, CA, and this website. We are the data controller for all information described here. Our Data Protection Officer can be reached any time at privacy@campquest.example.

02Data We Collect

From parents and guardians

Name, email, phone number, home city and billing details when you create an account or enroll a child. Payment card numbers never touch our servers — they are tokenized by our PCI-DSS Level 1 payment processor.

About campers

Only what camp safety genuinely requires: first name, age group, health forms (allergies, medications, emergency contacts) and pickup authorizations. Health records are stored separately with stricter access controls.

Technical data

IP address, browser type and pages visited, used solely for security monitoring and aggregate statistics.

03How We Use It

  • Running enrollments, schedules, payments and the Parent Dashboard feed
  • Daily photo digests and direct counselor messaging you have opted into
  • Safety-critical communication during camp hours, including emergencies
  • Anonymized reporting that helps us improve programs (never sold, never ad-targeted)

We do not use camper data for marketing, and we never profile children.

04Cookies & Analytics

This site uses a strictly necessary session cookie to keep you logged in, plus a single preference cookie remembering your light/dark and RTL choices. Our privacy-friendly analytics counts page visits without fingerprints or cross-site tracking. You can clear both cookies any time in your browser settings.

05Photo & Media Policy

Photos of activities are shared only inside your private family feed by default. Photos appear in public galleries (website, social media, brochures) only when you toggle on "Photo sharing consent" in Settings — and you can switch it off instantly, which removes future public use without deleting memories already in your private feed.

06Sharing & Third Parties

We share the minimum necessary with vetted processors under contract: payment processing, email delivery and cloud hosting (all US/EU data-center certified). We disclose data to authorities only where legally compelled, and we publish transparency requests annually. We have never sold family data and never will.

07How Long We Keep Data

  • Account & enrollment records: 3 years after your last session (or on request)
  • Health forms: end of season + 30 days, unless medication logs require longer by law
  • Family feed photos: yours until you delete them; deleted means deleted within 72 hours
  • Anonymized statistics: kept indefinitely — they contain no personal data

08Your Parental Rights

You may review, correct, export or erase your family's data at any time from Settings → Data export, or by emailing privacy@campquest.example. California residents hold additional CCPA rights; EU/UK families enjoy full GDPR rights including portability and objection. We respond to every request within 30 days — usually much faster.

09Security Measures

All traffic is encrypted with TLS 1.3; data at rest uses AES-256. Staff access follows least-privilege roles, background-checked admins pass annual security training, and independent penetration tests run every year. If a breach ever affects you, we notify affected families directly within 72 hours.

10Changes & Contact

Material changes are announced by email 30 days before taking effect, and this page always shows the current version date at the top. Questions big or small are welcome: privacy@campquest.example · +1 (800) 555-0202 · Attn: Privacy Office, 4821 Lakeshore Trail, Boulder Creek, CA 95007.
