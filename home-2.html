How It Works

From First Hello to Final Campfire

Joining CampQuest is effortless — four simple steps and your explorer is packed for adventure.

Say Hello

Book a free campus tour or video call. Meet our directors, walk the docks and ask us anything.

Pick a Journey

Choose from four signature worlds and weekly themes — mix sports, arts, coding and nature freely.

Reserve & Relax

Secure the week with a deposit, pick a payment plan and manage everything from the Parent Dashboard.

Arrive & Shine

Welcome kit, buddy assignment, sunrise circle — and by Friday, a camper bursting with stories.

Age Groups

Just-Right Challenges for Every Stage

Dedicated zones, equipment and counselor teams keep every camper safely stretched — never stressed.

5–7

Little Explorers

Play-led discovery with shorter activity blocks, cozy quiet corners and a 1:5 counselor ratio for first-time campers.

8–10

Trail Seekers

Skill badges, team challenges and their first canoe paddle — independence grows one adventure at a time.

11–14

Summit Crew

Overnight expeditions, advanced labs and leadership ladders that prepare teens to return as future staff.
