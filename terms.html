01Acceptance Of Terms

By creating an account, enrolling a camper or using this website, you agree to these Terms on behalf of yourself and any minor camper you enroll (you confirm you are their parent or legal guardian). If anything here feels unclear, ask us before enrolling — we'd rather explain twice than surprise once.

02Accounts & Eligibility

  • You must be 18+ to create an account and enroll campers aged 5–14.
  • One family account per household; add unlimited sibling profiles inside it.
  • You're responsible for keeping login credentials confidential and for activity under your account.
  • We may suspend accounts involved in fraud, harassment or safety threats.

03Enrollment & Payment

A seat is reserved when the listed deposit ($50 per week, applied to tuition) clears. Full balance is due 30 days before the session start unless you've chosen an installment plan. Prices are shown in USD, exclude optional add-ons where marked, and include meals, snacks and equipment as described on each program page. Sibling discounts apply automatically to the lesser-priced enrolment.

04Refunds & Rescheduling

Our flexible promise

  • 100% refund if you cancel more than 14 days before your session starts.
  • 50% refund or full credit within 14–7 days of start.
  • Full credit (no expiry) inside 7 days — life happens.
  • Free date change once per camper per summer, any time before session start.

If CampQuest cancels a session for any reason, you choose: a full refund, credit with 10% bonus, or free transfer to another week. Credits never expire while your oldest child remains camper-age eligible.

05Health & Safety Obligations

You agree to complete health forms honestly, list all allergies and medications, and keep immunizations current per California guidance. Campers must be able to participate in group activities with the staffing ratios described. Our RN staff manage routine care; for emergencies we act in loco parentis and contact you immediately. Sunscreen application is supervised and documented.

06Camper Conduct

We keep camp kind-first: bullying, endangering behavior or repeated disruption triggers our three-step path — counselor conversation, director call with parents, then possible dismissal without refund for the remainder of the week. We always work restoratively first; dismissal is truly rare (three times in fifteen years).

07Media Consent

Your private family feed is automatic. Public use of your camper's image happens only with the consent toggle you control in Settings, and withdrawing it stops future public use immediately. Campers wearing "no-photo" wristbands (available free at check-in) are excluded from all photography regardless of settings.

08Liability & Insurance

We carry $5M general liability plus camper accident coverage, certificates available on request. Participation involves normal outdoor risks (scrapes, bug bites, character-building mud). You agree to the standard release included in enrollment paperwork, which does not waive rights against negligence to the fullest extent California law allows.

09Intellectual Property

The CampQuest name, logo, program names and website content belong to us; camper artwork and projects belong forever to the campers (and we think that's exactly right). Families may share their own feed photos freely — please just don't resell them or remove other children from context.

10Governing Law & Changes

These Terms follow the laws of the State of California, disputes handled in Santa Cruz County courts, though we'll always try coffee-and-conversation first. Material changes are emailed 30 days ahead; continuing to use services after changes take effect means you accept them. Current version date always appears at the top of this page.
