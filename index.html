A Day at CampQuest

Structured Freedom, From Sunrise to Campfire

No two days repeat. Here is how a typical day flows across our four activity worlds.

01

Sunrise Circle 8:00 AM

Flag ceremony, high-energy warm-up games and the legendary CampQuest chant to wake up every explorer.

02

Signature Activity Blocks 10:00 AM

Campers dive into their chosen track — sports drills, canvas art, robotics lab or trail expedition — with specialist coaches.

03

Lakeview Lunch 12:30 PM

Chef-prepared hot lunches under the pavilion, followed by quiet games, reading nooks and letter writing home.

04

Choose-Your-Own Stations 2:00 PM

Free-choice afternoon across climbing wall, canoe dock, drama stage, science shed or shaded craft teepees.

05

Campfire Finale 5:00 PM

Songs, skits and s'mores as we celebrate daily wins — then handoff with photos already in the parent app.
