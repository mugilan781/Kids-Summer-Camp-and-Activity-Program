CampQuest began as one family's lakeside dream and grew into the adventure home of more than 12,000 children — without ever losing the porch-light warmth it started with.

Our Story

Built By Parents Who Wanted Something Better

In 2011, Sarah and Marcus Bell couldn't find a camp that balanced real adventure with real care — so they sold their car, leased a tired lakeside property and built the camp they wished existed.

Fifteen years later their daughter leads the counselor program on the very dock where she took her first swim lesson.

  • Every program designed by certified educators, not marketers
  • Staff return rate above 85% — kids get familiar faces yearly
  • Profits reinvested into equipment, scholarships and staff training
  • 120 scholarship campers hosted every single season

Mission & Values

The Compass That Guides Every Decision

Four values painted on the dining-hall wall — and tested by every activity we run.

Safety First, Always

Ratios enforced, EpiPens staged, radios on every hip. Adventure should raise heartbeats — never blood pressure.

Brave Over Perfect

We cheer first tries louder than trophies. Messy pottery and wobbly canoe starts are exactly the point.

Crew Over Self

Buddies, flag duties and campfire skits teach what scoreboards can't — that we rise together.

Nature Is The Teacher

Unplugged hours outside every day. Mud washes off; wonder doesn't.

Milestones

From Tired Dock to Award-Winning Campus

The chapters that shaped CampQuest into what families trust today.

'11

The First Summer 2011

38 campers, two counselors, one leaking canoe named Gertrude — and a promise made around the first campfire.

'14

ACA Accreditation 2014

Earned American Camp Association accreditation after a record-perfect safety inspection across 300+ standards.

'18

Maker Lab & Aqua Center Open 2018

The robotics lab and heated aquatics center opened, letting us launch coding camps and Red-Cross swim certification.

'22

Parent Dashboard Launch 2022

Live photo feeds, instant messaging and cashless payments brought parents along for every moment.

'25

Regional Camp Of The Year 2025

Voted Regional Camp of the Year by the Western Camp Association — judged on programming, safety and parent satisfaction.

Safety & Trust

Accredited, Inspected & Obsessively Prepared

We hold ourselves to hospital-grade protocols so parents can breathe easy.

Triple-Layer Screening

Federal background checks, reference verification and scenario-based interviews for every hire — including returning staff.

Always-Reachable Care

Two RN-staffed wellness lodges, camp-wide radio network and direct-line messaging from your dashboard.

Aquatics Certified Daily

Lifeguards run buddy-check systems every 15 minutes; swim bands match tested ability levels, not age.

Allergy-Aware Kitchen

Nut-free prep lines, individualized placemats for flagged allergies and chef briefings refreshed each session.
